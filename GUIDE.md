# Nour’s Calendar — how to use it

## First time

1. Open the link on your iPhone **in Safari**.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow) → **Add to Home Screen**.
   Now it has its own icon and opens full-screen, like a normal app.

From then on, just tap the icon. You stay signed in.

## Putting in your shifts

**One day:** tap the day → tap a shift (Morning, Night, Off…). That's it, it saves itself.

Tap the same shift again to remove it.

**A whole rotation at once:** tap any day → **Repeat shift…**
- Pick the shift
- Choose **Rotation — X days on, Y days off** (for 4-on-4-off, put 4 and 4)
  or **Certain weekdays** if you always work the same days
- Set the from/until dates → **Apply**

A month of shifts in about five taps.

**Stayed late?** Tap the day and change the Starts/Ends times just for that day.
The hours total at the bottom updates.

## Notes and details

Every day has a **Notes for this day** box — handover, who you swapped with,
anything you want to remember. It's searchable later and it's private to you.

There's also a **Ward / unit** box on any day you're working.

## Events and appointments

Tap the **+** button (bottom right), or **Add event** inside a day.

Give it a title, a time (or tick All day), and pick **Remind me** — 1 hour
before, 1 day before, whatever suits. Add a location, notes and a colour if
you like.

## Getting reminders on your phone

Open **⚙ Settings** (top right).

**Add it to my iPhone Calendar** — tap it once and say yes. Your shifts and
events then show up inside the normal iPhone Calendar app and it alerts you
there, even if you never open this app.

> After you subscribe, go to iPhone **Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar
> Accounts**, tap the subscription, and make sure **Remove Alarms** is **off**.
> If it's on, iPhone throws the reminders away.

**Push notifications** — if this switch is available, turn it on and the app
itself will buzz you. It only works once the app is on your Home Screen.

**Alert me before a shift starts** — set how much warning you want. Two hours
is the default.

## Making it yours

⚙ Settings → **My shift types**. Rename them, change the colours, change the
default hours, add your own, delete ones you don't use. Tap **Done** to save.

You can also set whether the week starts on Monday or Sunday, and force light
or dark mode.

## Things worth knowing

- **It works with no signal.** Open it anywhere; anything you change uploads
  itself the moment you have data again. A little bar at the top tells you when
  something's waiting.
- **Nobody else can see it.** It's your login only. Not your family, not
  whoever finds the link.
- **It's saved on the server, not the phone.** New phone, cracked screen,
  cleared Safari — sign in and it's all still there.
- **Backup:** ⚙ Settings → *Download a backup copy* saves a file of everything.

## If something looks wrong

Close the app and reopen it — it refreshes from the server every time.

If it says you're signed out, sign in again; nothing is lost.
